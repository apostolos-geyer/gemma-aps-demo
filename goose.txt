The Canada goose, *Branta canadensis*, is a large waterfowl species native to North America. They are easily recognizable by their black head and neck, white patches on the face, and brown body. It's a common sight to see them in Hamilton, Ontario, especially near the waterfront and in parks. While some people appreciate their presence as a part of the local wildlife, others find them to be a nuisance due to their droppings and sometimes aggressive behavior, particularly during nesting season.
